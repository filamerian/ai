Doctor of Education (EdD) Research Study

Artificial Intelligence Competency and Faculty Capacity for Digital Change Adaptation Among College Faculty Members of Filamer Christian University, Inc.

You are invited to participate in this research study conducted as part of the requirements for the degree Doctor of Education (EdD) major in Educational Leadership.

The purpose of this study is to examine the relationship between faculty members' Artificial Intelligence (AI) Competency and their Capacity for Digital Change Adaptation. Findings from this research may contribute to institutional planning, faculty development programs, and evidence-based policies for the effective integration of Artificial Intelligence in higher education.
