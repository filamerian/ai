Masterclass Overview

Navigating Generative AI with Academic Rigor

Generative AI tools are reshaping academic inquiry at an unprecedented pace. This masterclass provides faculty, researchers, and university scholars with a practical, policy-aligned framework for integrating Generative AI responsibly into scholarly workflows.

Rather than replacing scientific inquiry, Generative AI must serve as an assistance spectrum anchored in systematic verification, strict privacy compliance, and absolute researcher accountability.

Comprehensive Framework 4 Core Modules & Interactive Labs

Rigorous Verification

Strategies to combat hallucinations, audit citation provenance, and validate AI-generated synthesis against primary peer-reviewed literature.

Data Privacy & Protection

Safeguarding sensitive participant data, proprietary datasets, and intellectual property when interacting with commercial or open models.

Academic Integrity

Navigating ethical boundaries, authorship criteria, publisher guidelines, and institutional policies on Generative AI deployment.

Standardized Disclosure

Mastering structured AI declaration forms, transparency matrices, and standardized disclosure statements for journals and grant funding.

Core Academic Declaration

“AI ASSISTS.
THE RESEARCHER REMAINS ACCOUNTABLE.”

“Generative AI can support research tasks, but researchers remain responsible for verifying information, protecting data, applying appropriate methods, exercising judgment, and transparently disclosing AI use.”
